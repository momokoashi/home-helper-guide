☕ Cold Brew every day ready in ~12 hrs

  1. Add coffee grounds + cold water to the jug (about 1 part coffee to 8 parts water).
  2. Stir, cover, and leave it ~12 hours — overnight is perfect.
  3. Strain out the grounds — ready to pour. Keep in the fridge and make a fresh batch each day.

🧹 Cleaning keep it basic

  • Tidy anything lying around — put things back in their place.
  • Make the bed (quick is totally fine).
  • Do the laundry → hang it outside to dry.
  • The next day: bring it in & fold.

The three to start with. Make extra so there's always something ready.

Breakfast

💪 Hitting ~14–18g protein per breakfast portion: the main protein comes from our soy protein powder — use about 1 scoop per portion and check the tub's label so each serving lands around 14–18g. Tip: use soy milk (not oat milk) — it has more protein.

🥣 Overnight Oats

~14–18g protein / portion 5 min + chill makes 4 · keeps 3–4 days

Ingredients (4 portions)

  • 1½ cups rolled oats
  • 4 scoops our soy protein (1 per portion)
  • 2½ cups soy milk
  • 4 tbsp chia seeds
  • Fruit to top (banana, berries)

Steps

  1. Mix oats, soy protein, chia & soy milk in a bowl until no lumps.
  2. Divide into 4 jars, cover, chill overnight (4–5 hrs+).
  3. Each morning: stir, add a splash of milk if thick, top with fruit.

🫛 Soy Protein Jelly (pudding)

~14–18g protein / portion 10 min + set makes 4 · keeps 3–4 days

Ingredients (4 cups)

  • 2½ cups soy milk
  • 4 scoops our soy protein (1 per portion)
  • 1 packet gelatin (or 1 tsp agar for vegan)
  • Optional: a little honey/vanilla, fruit on top

Steps

  1. Warm half the soy milk (don't boil); whisk in the gelatin until dissolved.
  2. Whisk the soy protein into the cool half until smooth, then combine.
  3. Pour into 4 cups, chill 2 hrs+ until set. Top with fruit.

🦐 Easy & Quick Dinner — Garlic-Lemon Shrimp Stir-Fry

~22–25g protein / portion ~15 min scale up to batch for 2 days

Ingredients (2 portions)

  • Shrimp, peeled (fish market)
  • Garlic + ginger
  • Bell pepper + snow peas (or any market veg)
  • Olive oil, lemon, pinch of chili flakes, a little salt
  • Serve with rice

Steps

  1. Heat oil; add garlic & ginger, then the vegetables — stir-fry 2–3 min.
  2. Add the shrimp and cook just 2–3 min until pink (don't overcook — they turn rubbery).
  3. Finish with lemon juice & chili. Serve over rice. To batch: just double or triple it.
