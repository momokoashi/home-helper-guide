☕ Cold Brew every day ready in ~12 hrs

  1. Add coffee grounds + cold water to the jug (about 1 part coffee to 8 parts water).
  2. Stir, cover, and leave it ~12 hours — overnight is perfect.
  3. Strain out the grounds — ready to pour. Keep in the fridge and make a fresh batch each day.

🧹 Cleaning keep it basic

  • Tidy anything lying around — put things back in their place.
  • Make the bed (quick is totally fine).
  • Do the laundry → hang it outside to dry.
  • The next day: bring it in & fold.

That's it — nothing heavy 🙂

🌅 Breakfast batch & freeze a few portions

① Overnight Oats Rolled oats + our soy protein + soy/oat milk + chia. Mix, divide into jars, chill overnight. Top with fruit.
② Soy Protein Pudding (jelly) Soy milk + our soy protein + a little gelatin. Whisk smooth, pour into cups, chill until set.
