🌅 The two breakfasts to start with
💪 Aim for ~14–18g protein per portion: use about 1 scoop of our soy protein per serving (check the tub's label), and use soy milk — it has more protein than oat milk.

🥣 Overnight Oats

~14–18g protein / portion 5 min + chill makes 4

Ingredients (4 portions)

  • 1½ cups rolled oats
  • 4 scoops our soy protein (1 per portion)
  • 2½ cups soy milk
  • 4 tbsp chia seeds · fruit to top

Steps

  1. Mix oats, soy protein, chia & soy milk until no lumps.
  2. Divide into 4 jars, cover, chill overnight (4–5 hrs+).
  3. Each morning: stir, add a splash of milk, top with fruit.

🫛 Soy Protein Jelly (pudding)

~14–18g protein / portion 10 min + set makes 4

Ingredients (4 cups)

  • 2½ cups soy milk
  • 4 scoops our soy protein (1 per portion)
  • 1 packet gelatin (or 1 tsp agar)
  • Optional: a little honey/vanilla + fruit

Steps

  1. Warm half the soy milk (don't boil); whisk in gelatin until dissolved.
  2. Whisk soy protein into the cool half until smooth, then combine.
  3. Pour into 4 cups, chill 2 hrs+ until set. Top with fruit.
