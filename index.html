🫙 The batch-cooking idea

Cook once, eat for a few days.

  • Breakfast: make 3–4 portions (+ a little extra) so it's ready to grab.
  • Lunch & dinner: make a couple of dishes in bigger batches to eat over 2–3 days.
  • Chicken keeps well (~4 days in the fridge, and freezes) — great for big batches.
  • Squid & shrimp are best fresh — cook smaller amounts and eat within 1–2 days.
  • We have an oven — perfect for easy hands-off batches (tray bakes, baked oats).
  • Whenever the cleaning is lighter, that's a great time to batch-cook extra.

🫛 Soy Protein Milk Jelly (pudding-style)

~18–20g protein 10 min + set keeps 3–4 days

You need (makes ~4 cups)

  • 2 cups (500 ml) soy milk
  • 2–3 scoops our soy protein powder
  • 1 packet unflavoured gelatin OR 1 tsp agar powder
  • Optional: a little honey or vanilla, fresh fruit on top

Steps

  1. Warm half the soy milk (do not boil). Sprinkle the gelatin/agar over it and whisk until fully dissolved.
  2. Whisk the soy protein powder into the cool half until smooth and lump-free.
  3. Combine, add honey/vanilla if using, and pour into 4 small cups.
  4. Chill at least 2 hours or overnight until set like a soft jelly.
  5. Top with fruit before serving. Keep covered in the fridge.

📖 Reference: GreenLiteBites — Protein Jello · Hayl's Kitchen — Protein Pudding

🥣 High-Protein Overnight Oats

~30g protein 5 min + set keeps 3–4 days

You need (makes ~4 jars)

  • 2 cups rolled oats (old-fashioned, not instant)
  • 4 scoops our soy protein powder
  • 3 cups soy milk or oat milk
  • 4 tbsp chia seeds
  • Fruit to top: banana, berries

Steps

  1. Mix oats, protein powder, chia seeds and milk in a big bowl until no lumps remain.
  2. Divide into 4 jars, cover, and refrigerate overnight (at least 4–5 hours).
  3. Each morning, stir, add a splash of milk if thick, and top with fruit.

📖 Reference: The Real Food Dietitians — Protein Overnight Oats

🧁 Baked Protein Oatmeal Cups (oven · best for batching)

~12–15g protein each 30 min keeps ~1 week · freezes

You need (makes ~10 cups)

  • 3 cups rolled oats
  • 3 scoops our soy protein powder
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs · 2 cups soy/oat milk · 1 tsp baking powder
  • Optional: berries, a little honey, cinnamon

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F. Line a muffin tray.
  2. Mix everything in a bowl until combined.
  3. Spoon into the muffin cups and bake ~20–25 min until set and golden.
  4. Cool, then store in the fridge (1 week) or freezer. Grab-and-go breakfast.

Great use of the oven — make a big batch once and they last all week.

🍰 Protein Banana Breakfast Cake (oven · best for batching)

~12–15g protein / slice 35 min keeps ~4–5 days · freezes

You need (makes ~8 slices)

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs · ½ cup soy/oat milk
  • 2–3 scoops our soy protein powder
  • 1½ cups oat flour (or blend rolled oats into flour)
  • 1 tsp baking powder · 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: a little honey, berries, or dark chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F and line a loaf or cake tin.
  2. Whisk the mashed banana, eggs, milk (and honey if using) until smooth.
  3. Stir in the protein powder, oat flour, baking powder and cinnamon until just combined.
  4. Pour into the tin, add berries/chips on top, and bake ~25–30 min until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool, then slice. Store in the fridge (~4–5 days) or freeze slices — a grab-and-go breakfast.

📖 Reference: Unbound Wellness — Banana Protein Breakfast Cake · Chocolate Covered Katie — Protein Banana Bread

🍌 Warm Protein Oatmeal (fresh, for variety)

~25g protein 10 min made fresh

You need (1 portion)

  • ½ cup rolled oats + 1 cup water or soy milk
  • 1 scoop our soy protein powder
  • ½ banana, mashed · 1 tsp peanut butter (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the oats with water/milk for ~5 minutes until soft.
  2. Take off the heat, let it cool 1 minute, then stir in the protein powder (so it stays creamy, not grainy).
  3. Stir in banana, top with peanut butter or fruit, serve warm.

🍗 Oven Tray-Bake: Garlic-Lemon Chicken & Roasted Veg (oven · best for batching)

~40g protein 35 min keeps ~4 days · freezes

You need

  • 4–6 chicken breasts (Donki)
  • Vegetables that roast well: peppers, broccoli, carrots, zucchini (street market)
  • Olive oil, lots of garlic, lemon, black pepper, mixed herbs, a little salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F.
  2. Toss chicken and chopped vegetables on a tray with olive oil, garlic, herbs, pepper and lemon.
  3. Bake ~25 min until the chicken is cooked through (no pink inside).
  4. Cool, then divide into containers. Eat over ~4 days, or freeze portions.

📖 Reference: Beat The Budget — High Protein Chicken Meal Prep

🌿 Lemon-Herb Baked Chicken with Rice

~35g protein 25 min keeps ~4 days

You need

  • 4 chicken breasts (Donki)
  • Rice (brown rice is great), cooked
  • Lemon, garlic, mixed herbs, olive oil, black pepper, a little salt to taste
  • A side of steamed greens

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon, garlic, herbs & a little olive oil (10 min if there's time).
  2. Bake at 200°C / 400°F until cooked through and juicy.
  3. Rest 3 min, slice, and pack with rice + greens. Light and not salty.

📖 Reference: Nourish Move Love — High Protein Chicken Recipes

🍯 Honey-Garlic Chicken with Rice

~35g protein 25 min keeps ~4 days

You need

  • 4 chicken breasts, cubed
  • Rice, cooked
  • Glaze: garlic, 1 tbsp honey, a splash of low-sodium soy sauce, sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Pan-cook the chicken cubes in a little oil until golden and cooked through.
  2. Add garlic, honey, the soy sauce and sesame seeds; toss 2–3 min until glossy.
  3. Serve over rice with a side of vegetables. Pack into containers.

📖 Reference: Healthy Fitness Meals — Honey Garlic Chicken

🦑 Garlic, Chili & Parsley Squid

~18g protein 15 min best fresh · 1–2 days

You need

  • Fresh squid, cleaned (fish market)
  • Garlic, fresh red chili, parsley, good olive oil, lemon, a little salt to taste

Steps

  1. Pat the squid dry and score lightly (or use rings).
  2. Sear in a hot pan with olive oil for 1–2 minutes per side only (longer makes it rubbery).
  3. Add garlic, chili and parsley for the last 30 seconds.
  4. Squeeze lemon over and serve with a green salad or steamed vegetables.

📖 Reference: Cooked & Loved — Grilled Squid, Garlic, Chili & Parsley

🥬 Squid & Bok Choy Stir-Fry

~20g protein 20 min best fresh · 1–2 days

You need

  • Squid, cleaned & scored (fish market)
  • Bok choy, ginger, garlic, spring onion (street market)
  • A little sesame oil and low-sodium soy sauce (garlic & ginger add flavour too)

Steps

  1. Blanch the squid in boiling water for ~30 seconds, then drain.
  2. Stir-fry ginger & garlic, add bok choy until just wilted.
  3. Return the squid, toss with a little sesame oil and soy sauce for 1 minute, finish with spring onion. Serve hot.

📖 Reference: Omnivore's Cookbook — Squid & Bok Choy Stir-Fry

🦐 Garlic-Lemon Shrimp & Vegetable Stir-Fry

~22–25g protein 15 min best fresh · 1–2 days

You need

  • Shrimp, peeled (fish market)
  • Snow peas, bell pepper, ginger, garlic (street market)
  • Olive oil, lemon juice, pinch of chili flakes, a little salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil, add garlic & ginger, then the vegetables; stir-fry 2–3 min.
  2. Add shrimp and cook just 2–3 minutes until pink (don't overcook — they turn rubbery).
  3. Finish with lemon juice and chili flakes. Serve with rice or on its own.

📖 Reference: Clean Food Crush — Healthy Shrimp Recipes

🦑 Chilled Squid Salad (easy to pack)

~18–25g protein 20 min best fresh · 1–2 days

You need

  • Fresh squid, cleaned & sliced into rings (fish market)
  • Lemon juice, celery, red onion, parsley, a little olive oil, a little salt to taste
  • Optional: cherry tomatoes, cucumber

Steps

  1. Boil the squid rings 1–2 minutes only, then drain and cool.
  2. Toss with lemon juice, chopped vegetables, parsley and olive oil.
  3. Chill before serving — the lemon and a little salt bring it together. Packs well cold.

📖 Reference: Skinnytaste — Chilled Calamari Salad
